About

I’m Matthew Marquise, 24 years old, and based in Raleigh. I design the interface and build the website or software behind it.

How I work

I start with the person using the product. I look at what already exists, sketch alternatives, and keep the technical constraints in the room while the interface is still cheap to change. The goal is work that is clear for people and practical to ship.

Outside the work

Tennis, design events, pizza, photography, and movies, usually while trying to figure out how a shot was made.

Let’s build something clear.

I’m open to full-stack, front-end, and design roles. If you want to talk through a product, or a site that needs a calmer interface, write me.
